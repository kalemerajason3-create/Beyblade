Beyblade 101

This page will introduce you to the basics of Beyblade

Lets Begin

What are Beyblades?

Beyblades, referred to as Beys for short are tiny spinning tops made from plastic or metal for you to go and battle people with. The reason for their growth into a multimedia monster isn't just because the battles themselves are really fun with friends, but also because of their customizability and character. A Beyblade is made up of multiple components (differening between generations) that affect the way your tops behave in a battle, encouraging players to collect multiple of them so that they can build the strongest combo to beat their friends. The beys are based of creatures and characters, each from distinct cultural folklore, constellations, and ancient pantheons. This allows the players to become more emotionally attached to their partners to make the battles feel more epic than just two pieces of plastic hitting each other

How to Play

I order to play Beyblade you need at least 2 fully customized beys, one Beystadium (a bowl-shaped battlefield), and two launchers (String or Ripcord). You attach your bey onto the prongs of the launcher and point it into the stadium. When you and your opponent are ready you count down saying; 3 2 1... Go Shoot! or 3 2 1... Let it Rip! then simultaneously launch your bey into the stadium by pulling on the string/cord, activating the gear mechanism inside the launcher and unleashing you spinning top into the battle. They way you win is by outlasting your opponent, knocking your opponent out of the stadium, or in later installments, causing the opponents beyblade to burst (explode into its tiny pieces). In the event of a draw no points are given and the battle continues

Spin Direction and Types

Beyblades are divided by their types and spin direction. Beyblades can either spin clockwise (right) or counter-clockwise (left). They can also be put into 4 different types; Attack, Stamina, Defense, and Balance. Attack types move the fastest and deal the most damage, but have the worst stamina. Stamina types can outlast other beys, but they lack defensive power. Defense types are the best at taking hits, but they lack the ability to dish out those hits themselves. These 3 types have a Rock-Paper-Scissors relationship with each other. Attack beats Stamina, Stamina beats Defense, and Defense beats Attack. Balance is completely neutral, having characteristics of multiple types, thus having no clear strengths or weaknesses
